# UK Public Notices - Categorization & Workflow Requirements

Based on UK statutory requirements research (2024/2025)

---

## 📋 APPLICATION-BASED NOTICES
**Workflow:** External applicant → Council reviews → Approves → Publishes
**Platform Revenue:** Applicants pay submission fee + council subscription

### 1. **Licensing - Alcohol & Entertainment**

#### Premises Licence (New Application)
- **Who Submits:** Business owner, solicitor, or agent
- **Who Reviews:** Council Licensing Team
- **Statutory Timeline:** 28 days for representations
- **Legal Basis:** Licensing Act 2003
- **Notice Requirements:**
  - Blue A4 notice at premises (28 days)
  - Local newspaper ad (within 10 working days)
  - Copies to responsible authorities (police, fire, environmental health)
- **✅ Needs Application Workflow**

#### Variation of Premises Licence
- **Who Submits:** Licence holder or agent
- **Who Reviews:** Council Licensing Team
- **Same requirements as above**
- **✅ Needs Application Workflow**

#### Minor Variation
- **Who Submits:** Licence holder
- **Who Reviews:** Council Licensing Team (simplified process)
- **Timeline:** 10 working days
- **✅ Needs Application Workflow**

#### Club Premises Certificate
- **Who Submits:** Qualifying club
- **Who Reviews:** Council Licensing Team
- **Same publicity as premises licence**
- **✅ Needs Application Workflow**

#### Provisional Statement
- **Who Submits:** Prospective applicant (before premises built)
- **Who Reviews:** Council Licensing Team
- **✅ Needs Application Workflow**

---

### 2. **Goods Vehicle Operator Licences**

#### Goods Vehicle Operator Licence (New/Variation)
- **Who Submits:** Haulage company, transport business
- **Who Reviews:** Traffic Commissioner (not council, but same workflow)
- **Statutory Timeline:** Advertisement and application date must be within 21 days
- **Notice Requirements:**
  - Local newspaper circulating near operating centre
  - Traffic Commissioner publishes in "Notices and Proceedings" weekly
- **Legal Basis:** Goods Vehicles (Licensing of Operators) Act 1995
- **✅ Needs Application Workflow**
- **Special Note:** DVSA compliance, not council, but same submission model

---

### 3. **Planning Applications**

#### Planning Permission (Full/Outline)
- **Who Submits:** Developer, homeowner, architect
- **Who Reviews:** Council Planning Department
- **Statutory Timeline:** 21 days for comments (14 days for newspaper)
- **Notice Requirements:**
  - Neighbor notification letters (council sends)
  - Site notice (increasingly applicant responsibility)
  - Newspaper ad for major developments
- **Legal Basis:** Town and Country Planning Act 1990
- **✅ Needs Application Workflow**
- **Note:** Council publishes notices after receiving application

---

### 4. **Street Trading**

#### Street Trading Licence
- **Who Submits:** Vendor, market trader
- **Who Reviews:** Council Licensing Team
- **Statutory Timeline:** 28 days for objections (from newspaper ad)
- **Notice Requirements:**
  - Site notice at proposed trading location (21 days)
  - Targeted consultation with nearby businesses
- **✅ Needs Application Workflow**

---

### 5. **Probate Notices** (Partial Application Model)

#### Deceased Estates Notice (Trustee Act Notice)
- **Who Initiates:** Personal Representatives / Executors / Solicitors
- **Who Publishes:** The Gazette (official publication) + local newspaper
- **Statutory Timeline:** 2 months + 1 day before distributing estate
- **Legal Basis:** Trustee Act 1925
- **Notice Requirements:**
  - London Gazette (national)
  - Local newspaper where deceased lived
- **🟡 HYBRID Model:**
  - Executors/solicitors pay to publish notice
  - No council approval needed
  - **Platform Revenue:** Direct payment for publication service

## 🏛️ COUNCIL-INITIATED NOTICES
**Workflow:** Council creates → Publishes directly → Public comments
**Platform Revenue:** Council subscription only

### 1. **Traffic & Highways**

#### Traffic Regulation Orders (TRO)
- **Who Initiates:** Local Highway Authority (county/unitary council)
- **Who Publishes:** Council Highways Department
- **Statutory Timeline:** 21 days for objections
- **Legal Basis:** Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984
- **Notice Requirements:**
  - Local newspaper (Notice of Intention)
  - Site notices on affected roads
  - Consultation with emergency services
- **❌ No External Application**
- **Use Case:** Road closures, parking restrictions, speed limits

#### Experimental Traffic Orders
- **Same as TRO** - Council-initiated
- **❌ No External Application**

#### Permanent Traffic Orders
- **Same as TRO** - Council-initiated
- **❌ No External Application**

---

### 2. **Compulsory Purchase Orders (CPO)**

#### Compulsory Purchase Order
- **Who Initiates:** Council (local authority)
- **Who Approves:** Secretary of State
- **Statutory Timeline:** 21 days for objections (from newspaper notice)
- **Legal Basis:** Acquisition of Land Act 1981
- **Notice Requirements:**
  - Two successive weeks in local newspaper
  - Direct service on owners/lessees/occupiers
  - Public inquiry if objections
- **❌ No External Application**
- **Use Case:** Acquiring land for development, regeneration

---

### 3. **Tree Preservation Orders (TPO)**

#### Tree Preservation Order (New/Variation)
- **Who Initiates:** Council Planning Department (can be prompted by request)
- **Who Publishes:** Council
- **Statutory Timeline:** 28 days for objections, 6 months to confirm
- **Legal Basis:** Town and Country Planning Act 1990
- **Notice Requirements:**
  - Notify owners, occupants, known tree surgeons
  - Parish councils
- **❌ No External Application** (though can be prompted by resident request)
- **Use Case:** Protecting significant trees

---

### 4. **Environmental Health Orders**

#### Noise Abatement Notices
- **Who Initiates:** Council Environmental Health
- **❌ No External Application**

#### Food Hygiene Emergency Closures
- **Who Initiates:** Council Environmental Health
- **❌ No External Application**

## 📊 BUSINESS MODEL IMPLICATIONS

### **Application Portal Model (Recommended):**

#### Revenue Streams:
1. **Council Subscriptions** (Monthly/Annual)
   - Per department access
   - Unlimited notice publications
   - Analytics and compliance tools
   - £500-2,000/month per department

2. **Applicant Submission Fees** (Per Application)
   - Licensing applications: £50-150 per submission
   - Planning applications: £75-200 per submission
   - GV operator licences: £100-250 per submission
   - Street trading: £30-75 per application
   - Probate notices: £50-150 per notice

3. **Solicitor/Agent Accounts** (Monthly)
   - Multi-client management
   - Application tracking dashboard
   - £200-500/month for firms

#### Market Size (UK):
- **343 Local Authorities** (England)
- **~50-100 licensing applications per council per year** = 17,150-34,300 applications
- **~1,000-3,000 planning applications per council per year** = 343,000-1,029,000 applications
- **~20-50 GV operator licences per Traffic Commissioner area** = 1,600-4,000 applications
- **Thousands of solicitors/agents** submitting on behalf of clients

#### Revenue Potential:
- **Council subscriptions**: 343 councils × 2-5 departments × £1,000/mo = £686,000-£1,715,000/mo
- **Application fees**: Conservative 10% market penetration
  - Licensing: 3,000 × £100 = £300,000/year
  - Planning: 50,000 × £150 = £7,500,000/year
  - GV Operator: 500 × £150 = £75,000/year
  - Probate: 5,000 × £100 = £500,000/year

**Total Potential: £8M-£21M annual revenue**

---

### **Publishing-Only Model (Less Revenue):**

#### Revenue Streams:
1. **Council Subscriptions Only**
   - £500-2,000/month per department
   - No application fees

2. **One-off Publication Fees**
   - Councils pay per notice published
   - £20-50 per notice

#### Market Limitations:
- Only captures council-initiated notices (TROs, CPOs, TPOs)
- Misses high-volume application market
- Lower value proposition

**Total Potential: £2M-£5M annual revenue** (significantly less)

## ✅ RECOMMENDATION: HYBRID SYSTEM

### **Keep Your Existing UI, Add Application Workflow:**

#### For Application-Based Notices (60-70% of volume):
- Use existing PublishPage form
- Add "Select Target Council" dropdown for applicants
- Add submission workflow (firm → council → approve → publish)
- Charge applicants submission fee
- Track SLA compliance for councils

#### For Council-Initiated Notices (30-40% of volume):
- Use same PublishPage form
- Council users bypass submission workflow
- Direct publish to public
- No application fee (included in subscription)
