# Avatar Profiles - User Personas

**Purpose**: Define the needs, workflows, and portal requirements for every user type across council and law firm portals.

# COUNCIL AVATARS

## 1. Licensing Head of Department

### Profile
- **Name**: Sarah Mitchell
- **Role**: Head of Licensing, Westminster Council
- **Experience**: 15 years in local government licensing
- **Team Size**: 8 officers (2 senior, 4 mid-level, 2 junior)
- **Workload**: 150-200 active applications per month

### Daily Workflow
1. **Morning**: Review overnight representations, check consultation deadlines
2. **Mid-day**: Assign cases to officers, review complex applications
3. **Afternoon**: Prepare committee reports, respond to urgent queries
4. **End-of-day**: Check processing times, update performance metrics

### Pain Points with Current Systems
- Idox is slow and clunky for viewing online notices
- No dashboard view of consultation deadlines
- Manual tracking of representation counts
- Difficult to assign cases to team members
- No way to see which notices are getting most public attention

### What Sarah Needs from Our Portal

#### Dashboard Must Show:
- **Active Applications by Type**: New premises, variations, reviews, transfers
- **Urgent Deadlines**: Consultations ending in next 7 days (red alert), 14 days (amber)
- **Representation Activity**: New reps today, this week, trending notices
- **Processing Metrics**:
  - Average time from publication to decision
  - % of applications meeting statutory deadlines
  - Officer workload distribution
- **Notice Type Breakdown**: Premises licenses, club licenses, personal licenses, TENs
- **Status Overview**: Published, under consultation, closed, decided

#### Key Features Required:
- View all notices for Licensing department only (filtered automatically)
- Click any notice → see full details, all representations, timeline
- **Mark representations as "Reviewed"** with reviewer name and date
- **Assign to team member** with notification to that officer
- **Add internal notes** (visible only to Licensing team, not public)
- **Export for Idox**: Download representations as CSV with all fields for import
- **Search/filter notices**: By applicant name, premises address, notice type, date range
- **Sort representations**: By date, stance (support/object/comment), reviewed status

#### Success Metrics:
- Can review all new representations in under 30 minutes
- Zero missed consultation deadlines
- All representations assigned within 24 hours
- Complete audit trail of who reviewed what and when

## 2. Planning Head of Department

### Profile
- **Name**: David Chen
- **Role**: Head of Planning, Westminster Council
- **Experience**: 20 years in planning, 5 years as head
- **Team Size**: 15 officers (3 senior planners, 8 planners, 4 admin)
- **Workload**: 300-400 applications per month across all types

### Daily Workflow
1. **Morning**: Check major applications, neighbour notification status
2. **Site visits**: 2-3 per week for complex applications
3. **Committee prep**: Weekly planning committee reports
4. **Public engagement**: Respond to councillor queries, resident concerns

### Pain Points
- Planning applications more complex than licensing (drawings, site plans, conditions)
- Need to track neighbour notifications separately from site notices
- Multiple consultation types (statutory consultees, public, councillors)
- Need to cross-reference with conservation areas, listed buildings

### What David Needs

#### Dashboard Must Show:
- **Application Types**:
  - Full planning permission
  - Outline planning
  - Listed building consent
  - Advertisement consent
  - Tree preservation orders
  - Lawful development certificates
- **Statutory Deadlines**: 8 weeks for major, 6 weeks for minor, extensions
- **Site Notice Status**: Published, expired, evidence uploaded
- **Neighbour Notification Tracking**: How many sent, responses received
- **Consultee Responses**: Heritage, highways, environmental health responses
- **Ward Analysis**: Applications by ward for councillor briefings
- **Appeal Risk**: Applications approaching decision deadline

#### Key Features Required:
- All features from Licensing PLUS:
- **Document attachments**: Plans, drawings, heritage statements
- **Map integration**: Show application site with red line boundary
- **Consultee tracking**: Mark which consultees have responded
- **Committee scheduling**: Flag applications for committee vs delegated
- **Site visit notes**: Internal notes from site inspections
- **Condition library**: Standard conditions by application type
- **Policy references**: Link to relevant UDP/Local Plan policies

#### Planning-Specific Metrics:
- Applications by ward
- Performance against 8/13 week targets
- Appeal rates and outcomes
- Major vs minor vs other splits
- Validation vs determination times

# LAW FIRM AVATARS

## 1. Licensing Solicitor

### Profile
- **Name**: Emma Watson (Partner)
- **Firm**: Wilson & Partners, London
- **Experience**: 12 years licensing law, 300+ applications
- **Caseload**: 15-20 active applications at any time
- **Practice**: 80% premises licensing, 20% personal/club licenses

### Daily Workflow
1. **Client meetings**: Take instructions, advise on process
2. **Application prep**: Draft operating schedules, plans
3. **Notice management**: Ensure proper publication, track responses
4. **Representation handling**: Respond to objections, negotiate with objectors
5. **Hearing prep**: If opposed, prepare for committee

### Pain Points with Current System
- Manually checking newspaper publication dates
- No way to know if notice was properly displayed
- Missing objection deadline because didn't check in time
- Client asks "has anyone objected?" - need to phone council
- Blue notice creation is manual, time-consuming
- No central place to see all client applications

### What Emma Needs from Our Portal

#### Firm Dashboard Must Show:
- **My Active Applications**: All current client applications, status at a glance
- **Upcoming Deadlines**:
  - Representation deadlines (21 days from publication)
  - Hearing dates
  - Decision dates
- **Alerts**: "New representation received on [client] application"
- **By Client**: Filter to see all applications for specific client (e.g., Pub Co with 50 pubs)
- **By Status**: Draft, published, under consultation, determined
- **This Month's Activity**: Applications submitted, hearings attended, notices published

#### Publishing Features Emma Needs:
1. **Select Notice Type**: New premises, variation, review, transfer
2. **Select Council**: Dropdown of all UK councils
3. **Fill Form**: Auto-populated from client data if repeat client
4. **Council Template Auto-Match**: System uses council's template automatically
5. **Preview**: See exactly what will be published
6. **Auto-Generate Blue Notice PDF**:
   - A4 size
   - Blue background
   - Contains all statutory information
   - QR code at bottom linking to online notice
   - Print-ready format
   - Instructions included: "Display this notice at the premises for 28 days"
7. **Payment**: Use firm's allowance (50 notices/month on Professional plan)
8. **Confirmation**: Email with notice link, blue notice PDF, tracking link

#### During Consultation Period:
- **Live Representation Feed**: See representations as they're submitted (it's public data anyway)
- **Representation Count**: "3 objections, 1 support, 0 comments"
- **Consultation End Date**: Countdown "5 days remaining"
- **Email Updates**: "New representation received" notifications

#### Communication Features (HubSpot-Style):
- **Email Tracking**: Track emails to/from licensing@westminster.gov.uk
- **Thread View**: See all correspondence about this application in one place
- **Response Status**: Council viewed? Council responded?
- **Note**: This requires email integration - probably for Phase 2, not MVP

#### Client Management:
- **Client Profiles**: Save client details (Pub Co Ltd, contact: John Smith)
- **Quick Publish**: "Publish new notice for [existing client]" - auto-fills their details
- **Client Dashboard**: Show client all their applications, representations received
- **White-label**: Optional branded view for client access

#### Blue Notice Generator Must Include:
```
[BLUE BACKGROUND PDF]

APPLICATION FOR A PREMISES LICENCE

Premises: The Red Lion, 123 High Street, London, SW1A 1AA

Notice is hereby given that [Applicant Name] has applied to
Westminster City Council for a premises licence under the
Licensing Act 2003.

The application is for:
[Operating Hours]
[Licensable Activities]

A copy of the application and plans can be viewed at:
[Council address]
Or online at: [URL with QR code]

Representations may be made to Westminster Licensing Authority
until: [Date - 21 days from publication]

[QR CODE - links to online notice where public can make representations]

Published: [Date]
Reference: [Notice ID]
```

#### Success Metrics for Emma:
- Can publish a notice in under 10 minutes (vs 45 minutes now)
- Zero missed representation deadlines
- Clients impressed by blue notice quality
- Saved 2 hours per application on admin
- Firm publishes 50 notices/month easily

# DEVELOPMENT PRIORITIES BASED ON AVATARS

## Phase 1 - MVP (Must Have)
Focus on Sarah (Licensing Council) and Emma (Licensing Firm) - they have the highest volume and clearest pain points.

1. **Licensing Council Portal**:
   - Dashboard with deadlines and rep counts
   - Assign to team member
   - Mark as reviewed
   - Export for Idox

2. **Licensing Firm Portal**:
   - Quick notice publishing
   - Blue notice PDF generation with QR
   - Live representation feed
   - Client management basics

## Phase 2 - Expansion
Add David (Planning) and Thomas (Planning Solicitor) - second highest volume.

3. **Planning Council Portal**:
   - Application type tracking
   - Map integration
   - Consultee management

4. **Planning Firm Portal**:
   - Site notice generation (A1 size)
   - Neighbour notification tracking
   - Document upload

## Phase 3 - Complete Coverage
Add all other departments and practice areas.

5. **All Other Councils**: Environmental Health, Highways, Building Control
6. **All Other Firms**: Property, Corporate/Insolvency
