## 💡 Pro Tips for Searching

1. **Use partial postcodes** for broader results:
   - "B1" finds all Birmingham B1 postcodes
   - "BN" finds all Brighton postcodes

2. **Combine search with filters**:
   - Search "Bristol" + Filter "Planning" = Planning applications in Bristol only

3. **Search by premises name**:
   - "The Railway Inn" - finds specific pub across any location
   - "Mill Quarter" - finds the specific development

4. **Use the map view**:
   - Switch to map view to see geographic distribution
   - Zoom into specific regions
   - Click clusters to see all notices in an area
