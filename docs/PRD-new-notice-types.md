# Product Requirements Document: New Notice Types for Civic Notices
**Version 1.0** | **Date: January 2026**

## 1. Executive Summary

Civic Notices is positioned to expand beyond statutory advertising to become the UK's leading platform for all civic and legal notice publications. This PRD outlines strategic expansion into seven high-value notice types representing a combined addressable market of **£45-60M annually**.

**Strategic Priority**: Focus on notice types that leverage existing strengths while targeting underserved segments.

## 2. Market Analysis

### Overall Market Context
- Total UK legal/civic notices market: ~£200M annually
- Current penetration: <5% of addressable market
- Growth drivers: Digital adoption, regulatory compliance, cost pressures

### Competition Landscape
- **TheLawyer-Locator**: Traditional, clunky, high fees
- **Local newspapers**: Fragmented, inconsistent
- **Whitehead & Co**: Niche probate, expensive
- **Planning portals**: Government-led but poor UX

## 4. Phase 1: Probate + Planning (Q1-Q2)

### Probate Notices (Trustee Act s.27)
- **Market**: ~108,000 notices annually
- **Price**: £199 all-inclusive vs £350+ competitors
- **Requirements**: London Gazette + local newspaper
- **Implementation**: London Gazette API, estate calculator, solicitor portal

### Planning Notices
- **Market**: ~100,000 notices requiring newspaper publication
- **Price**: £99 per notice
- **Requirements**: Local newspaper, 21-day consultation
- **Implementation**: Geographic targeting, developer portal

## 5. Phase 2: TRO + Gambling (Q3-Q4)

### Traffic Regulation Orders
- **Market**: ~25,000 TROs annually
- **Price**: £179 per notice
- **Requirements**: Local newspaper, 21-day objection period
- **Implementation**: Council portals, bulk processing

### Gambling Act Notices
- **Market**: ~5,000 applications annually
- **Price**: £299 premium service
- **Requirements**: Local newspaper, 28-day period
- **Implementation**: Gambling Commission compliance

## 6. Phase 3: Environmental + Path + CPO (2025)

### Environmental Notices
- **Market**: £1.3-3.4M for EIA notices
- **Price**: £249 per notice
- **Requirements**: EIA Regulations 2017, 30+ day consultation

### Public Path Orders
- **Market**: ~8,000 orders annually
- **Price**: £159 per notice
- **Requirements**: Highways Act 1980, mapping

### CPO Notices
- **Market**: ~1,000 major notices annually
- **Price**: £799 complex service
- **Requirements**: Multiple stages, landowner notification
