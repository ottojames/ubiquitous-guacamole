# Gambling Act 2005 - Notice Requirements Research

**Research Date:** January 2026  
**Purpose:** Statutory advertising requirements for gambling licence applications

---

## Overview

The Gambling Act 2005 regulates commercial gambling in Great Britain (England, Wales, and Scotland). Various applications and notices under the Act require public advertisement, primarily through:
1. **Local newspaper advertisements**
2. **Site notices displayed at premises**
3. **Notices to responsible authorities**

The primary regulations governing notice requirements are:
- **The Gambling Act 2005 (Premises Licences and Provisional Statements) Regulations 2007** (SI 2007/459)
- **The Gambling Act 2005 (Temporary Use Notices) Regulations 2007** (SI 2007/3157)

---

## 1. Premises Licence Applications

### Types of Premises Licences
- Casino Premises Licence
- Bingo Premises Licence
- Betting Premises Licence (including Tracks)
- Adult Gaming Centre (AGC) Premises Licence
- Family Entertainment Centre (FEC) Premises Licence

### Notice Requirements

#### A. Notice to Responsible Authorities
**Regulation:** SI 2007/459, Regulation 12(1)

- **Timing:** Within **7 days** beginning on the date the application is made
- **Recipients:** All responsible authorities under section 157 of the Act:
  - The Licensing Authority
  - The Gambling Commission
  - Chief Officer of Police
  - Fire and Rescue Service
  - Local Planning Authority
  - Environmental Health Authority
  - Child Protection/Safeguarding Board
  - HM Revenue & Customs
- **Form:** Prescribed Form A (single applicant) or Form B (multiple applicants) as set out in Schedule 6, Part 1

#### B. Local Newspaper Advertisement
**Regulation:** SI 2007/459, Regulation 12(6)(a)

| Requirement | Detail |
|-------------|--------|
| **Publication** | Local newspaper circulating in the licensing authority's area |
| **Alternative** | If no local newspaper: local newsletter, circular, or similar document |
| **Frequency** | At least **once** |
| **Timing** | During the period of **10 working days** starting with the day **after** the application is made |
| **Form** | Prescribed form in Schedule 6, Part 2 |

**Working Day Definition:** Not a Saturday, Sunday, Christmas Day, Good Friday, or bank holiday in England and Wales.

**Content of Notice Must Include:**
- Name of applicant(s)
- Address of premises
- Type of premises licence applied for
- Date of application
- Details of how representations can be made
- Period within which representations must be made (28 days)
- Address of licensing authority

#### C. Site Notice Display
**Regulation:** SI 2007/459, Regulation 12(6)(b)

| Requirement | Detail |
|-------------|--------|
| **Location** | At the premises, visible from the exterior |
| **Position** | Where it can conveniently be read by members of the public from outside |
| **Duration** | **28 consecutive days** starting on the day the application is made |
| **Size** | A4 minimum (recommended by most councils) |
| **Colour** | White background with black text (commonly required) |
| **Form** | Same prescribed form as newspaper advertisement (Schedule 6, Part 2) |

**Additional Site Notice Requirements for Large Premises:**
- If premises exceed **50 metres square**, additional notices must be displayed
- Further notices required every **50 metres** along the external perimeter where it opens onto a highway or public area
- All notices must be readable by passers-by

#### D. Representation Period
- **Duration:** 28 days from the date the application was made
- Responsible authorities and interested parties may make representations during this period

## 2. Variation of Premises Licence

### Notice Requirements
Same as new premises licence applications:

| Notice Type | Timing | Duration |
|-------------|--------|----------|
| Responsible Authorities | Within 7 days | N/A |
| Newspaper Advertisement | Within 10 working days | Once |
| Site Notice | From day of application | 28 consecutive days |

**Form:** Schedule 6, Parts 3 and 4 of SI 2007/459

---

## 3. Transfer of Premises Licence

### Notice Requirements (Reduced)
- **Notice to Responsible Authorities only** (subset):
  - Gambling Commission
  - Chief Officer of Police
  - Licensing Authority
  - HMRC
  - (Paragraphs (a), (b), (c), (h) and (i) of section 157)
- **Timing:** Within 7 days
- **No newspaper advertisement required**
- **No site notice required**

---

## 4. Reinstatement of Premises Licence

### Notice Requirements (Reduced)
- Same as transfer applications (limited responsible authorities only)
- **No newspaper advertisement required**
- **No site notice required**
- Must apply within **6 months** of the licence lapsing

---

## 5. Provisional Statements

### Purpose
For premises expected to be constructed, altered, or where the applicant expects to acquire a right to occupy.

### Notice Requirements
Same as new premises licence applications:
- Notice to responsible authorities within 7 days
- Newspaper advertisement within 10 working days
- Site notice for 28 consecutive days

**Special Provision:** If premises not yet constructed or applicant does not have right to occupy, the site notice must be displayed:
- As near as reasonably practicable to the proposed premises
- Where it can conveniently be read by members of the public

**Form:** Schedule 6, Parts 7 and 8 of SI 2007/459

## 6. Temporary Use Notices (TUNs)

**Regulation:** SI 2007/3157

### Purpose
Allows gambling operators with an operating licence to temporarily use premises (without a gambling premises licence) for gambling activities. Common venues: hotels, conference centres, sporting venues.

### Key Limitations
- Only **equal chance gaming** permitted (e.g., poker tournaments)
- Maximum **21 days** in any 12-month period per premises
- Only one activity may be specified at a time
- No bankers games allowed

### Notice Requirements

| Recipient | Timing |
|-----------|--------|
| Licensing Authority | **3 months and 1 day** before the event |
| Gambling Commission | Copy to be given |
| Chief Officer of Police | Copy to be given |
| HM Revenue & Customs | Copy to be given |

**Note:** No newspaper advertisement required. No public site notice required.

### Process
1. Notice given to licensing authority
2. Authority must acknowledge receipt
3. 14-day objection period
4. If no objections: authority endorses the notice (permission granted)
5. If objections: hearing within 6 weeks; authority may issue counter-notice

## 7. Occasional Use Notices (OUNs)

**Legislation:** Section 39 of the Gambling Act 2005

### Purpose
Permits betting on a **track** for up to **8 days** in a calendar year without requiring a full betting premises licence.

### Applicant
- Person responsible for administration of events on the track, OR
- Occupier of the track

### Notice Requirements

| Recipient | Notes |
|-----------|-------|
| Licensing Authority | Must specify the day(s) on which notice has effect |
| Chief Officer of Police | Copy to be given |

**Note:**
- No newspaper advertisement required
- No public site notice required
- No need to notify the Gambling Commission
- **No provision for objections** if within 8-day annual limit
- Notices may cover consecutive days

## 8. Licensing Authority's Statement of Principles

**Regulation:** SI 2006/636 (The Gambling Act 2005 (Licensing Authority Policy Statement)(England and Wales) Regulations 2006)

### Requirement
Licensing authorities must publish a Statement of Principles every **3 years** (as required by section 349 of the Act).

### Advertisement of Statement
Before a statement or revision comes into effect, the authority must publish a notice:

| Where | Duration |
|-------|----------|
| Authority's website | At least 4 weeks before coming into effect |
| **AND** at least one of: | |
| - Local newspaper circulating in the area | At least 4 weeks before coming into effect |
| - Local newsletter/circular | At least 4 weeks before coming into effect |
| - Public notice board (principal office) | At least 4 weeks before coming into effect |
| - Public libraries notice board | At least 4 weeks before coming into effect |

**Content of Notice:**
- Date statement/revision is to be published
- Date it will come into effect
- Internet address where it can be viewed
- Address of premises where it may be inspected

## Notes for Practice

1. **Proof of Publication:** A copy of the newspaper containing the advertisement should be provided to the licensing authority before the licence can be issued.

2. **Form Compliance:** Use the prescribed forms from Schedule 6 of SI 2007/459. Most licensing authorities provide template notices on their websites.

3. **Working Days Calculation:** Exclude weekends and bank holidays when calculating the 10 working day deadline for newspaper publication.

4. **Site Notice Guidance:** Many councils recommend:
   - A4 size minimum
   - White background, black text
   - Laminated or protected from weather
   - Positioned at eye level
   - Well-lit location

5. **Failure to Comply:** If proper notice is not given, the licensing authority cannot grant the application until proper notice has been given and a further 28-day representation period has elapsed.
