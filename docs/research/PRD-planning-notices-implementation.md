# PRD: Planning Notices Implementation (Phase 1)

**Target Launch**: Q1 2024 | **Revenue Target**: £990k annually | **Market Share Target**: 10%

## Executive Summary

Planning notices represent a massive volume opportunity within the statutory advertising market. With 500,000+ planning applications annually and 100,000+ requiring newspaper publication, this £10M+ addressable market offers high-volume, recurring revenue potential.

**Key Success Factors:**
- £99 per notice pricing beats fragmented market
- Single platform vs. scattered council/newspaper relationships
- Automated compliance with Town & Country Planning Act 1990
- Target: 100+ property developers and planning consultants

## 1. Market Opportunity

### Market Size Analysis
- **Total planning applications annually**: ~500,000 in England
- **Notices requiring publication**: ~100,000 (major developments)
- **Current pricing**: £80-£200 per notice
- **Our target price**: £99 per notice
- **Addressable market**: £9.9M annually

### Market Fragmentation Issues
**Current Problems**:
- Developers deal with 317+ different councils
- Each council has different newspaper relationships
- Inconsistent pricing across regions
- Manual processes cause delays
- No centralized tracking or compliance monitoring

**Types of Development Requiring Publication**:
- Major residential developments (10+ units)
- Commercial developments >1,000 sqm
- Developments in conservation areas
- Listed building applications
- Applications contrary to development plan
- EIA developments (covered separately)

## 2. Legal Framework & Requirements

### Town & Country Planning Act 1990
**Section 65**: Publicity requirements for planning applications
**Development Management Procedure Order 2015**: Specific publication requirements

### Publication Requirements

#### Major Developments
**Definition**: 
- Residential: 10+ dwelling units OR site area >0.5 hectares
- Commercial: Floor space >1,000 sqm OR site area >1 hectare
- Other developments exceeding specified thresholds

**Publication Requirements**:
- Local newspaper serving the area (minimum 1 insertion)
- Site notice on or near the land
- Council website publication
- 21-day consultation period minimum

## 8. Risk Management

### Market Risks
**Digital Planning Reforms**: Government push toward digital-only notices
- **Mitigation**: Position as bridge solution, develop digital-first offerings

**Economic Downturn**: Reduced development activity  
- **Mitigation**: Focus on essential development types, government infrastructure

### Operational Risks
**Newspaper Industry Consolidation**: Reduced publication options
- **Mitigation**: Develop alternative publication channels, digital supplements

**Volume Scaling Issues**: System unable to handle growth
- **Mitigation**: Cloud-native architecture, automated workflow design

### Competitive Risks
**New Market Entrants**: Technology companies entering space
- **Mitigation**: First-mover advantage, exclusive newspaper partnerships
