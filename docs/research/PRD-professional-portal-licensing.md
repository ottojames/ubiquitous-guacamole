# PRD: Professional Portal for Licensing Consultants

**Target Launch**: Q2 2024 | **Revenue Target**: £2.4M annually | **Pricing**: £200/month per consultant

## Executive Summary

The Professional Portal transforms Civic Notices from a transactional service into a comprehensive workflow management platform for licensing consultants. This SaaS offering targets the £200/month price point that provides massive ROI for consultants handling 20+ licensing applications annually.

**Market Opportunity**:
- 500-750 licensing consultants/firms nationwide
- Average 20-50 applications per consultant annually
- Current tools: Generic legal software + manual processes
- Target: 200 subscribers by end Year 1 = £480k ARR
- Full potential: 1,000+ subscribers = £2.4M ARR

## 1. Market Analysis & Justification

### Current Licensing Consultant Workflow
**Time per Application**: 8-12 hours total
1. **Client consultation**: 1-2 hours
2. **Application preparation**: 3-4 hours  
3. **Newspaper coordination**: 1-2 hours
4. **Council submission**: 1-2 hours
5. **Follow-up and completion**: 2-3 hours

**Pain Points**:
- Manual tracking across multiple applications
- Fragmented tools (legal software + spreadsheets + email)
- Deadline management stress
- Client communication overhead
- Newspaper advertising complexity
- Council-specific requirements

### Value Proposition for £200/Month
**Time Savings**: 3-5 hours per application
- **50 applications/year**: 150-250 hours saved
- **Consultant hourly rate**: £150-£300
- **Value created**: £22,500-£75,000 annually
- **ROI**: 1,100% - 3,700%

**Revenue Protection**: Deadline compliance
- **Missed deadline cost**: Entire application fee (£800-£2,000)
- **One prevented miss pays for**: 4-10 months subscription

## 2. Core Platform Features

### 2.1 Application Pipeline Management

#### Visual Pipeline Dashboard
**Stages**:
- **Lead/Enquiry**: Potential clients and projects
- **Quote/Proposal**: Pricing and scope discussions  
- **Instructed**: Confirmed work, payment received
- **In Progress**: Active application preparation
- **Submitted**: Awaiting council/newspaper processing
- **Published**: Newspaper publication live
- **Determined**: Council decision received
- **Completed**: Full cycle finished

**Kanban Interface**:
- Drag-and-drop stage progression
- Color-coded urgency (red: <7 days, amber: <14 days)
- Application value and status indicators
- Client communication history preview
- Next action requirements

### 2.2 Intelligent Workflow Automation

#### Deadline Management Engine
**Automated Calculations**:
- **28-day consultation periods** (from newspaper publication)
- **Council determination deadlines** (typically 8-12 weeks)
- **Appeal deadlines** (21 days from decision)
- **License renewal reminders** (annual license expiry tracking)

**Alert System**:
- **7-day warnings**: Email and dashboard notifications
- **24-hour urgency**: SMS and phone call escalation
- **Client notifications**: Automatic status updates
- **Missed deadline recovery**: Escalation protocols

#### Document Automation
**Template Library**:
- **Application forms**: Pre-populated council-specific templates
- **Client letters**: Status updates, information requests
- **Statutory notices**: Compliant legal wording
- **Invoice templates**: Automated billing with terms

### 2.3 Client Relationship Management

#### Client Portal Access
**White-label Branding**: Consultant's logo and styling
**Client Dashboard**:
- Application status tracking
- Document repository access
- Message center for communications
- Invoice history and payment portal
- Deadline calendar view

**Real-time Updates**:
- SMS notifications for status changes
- Email alerts for document requests
- Calendar invites for hearings/meetings
- Progress percentage indicators

### 2.4 Financial Management Integration

#### Pricing & Invoicing Engine
**Flexible Pricing Models**:
- **Fixed fee**: Standard pricing per license type
- **Hourly rate**: Time-based billing with tracking
- **Value-based**: Success fees and outcome pricing
- **Retainer**: Monthly recurring relationship fees

**Automated Billing**:
- **Milestone invoicing**: Payment at application stages
- **Expense tracking**: Newspaper costs, council fees
- **Payment processing**: Direct debit and online payments
- **Late payment management**: Automated reminders
