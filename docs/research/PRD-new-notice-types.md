# Product Requirements Document: New Notice Types for Civic Notices
**Version 1.0** | **Date: January 2024**

## 1. Executive Summary

Civic Notices is positioned to expand beyond our core offering of statutory advertising to become the UK's leading platform for all civic and legal notice publications. This PRD outlines our strategic expansion into seven high-value notice types that represent a combined addressable market of £45-60M annually.

**Key Opportunity**: While the legal notice market is fragmented with multiple players, Civic Notices has the unique advantage of:
- Existing relationships with local newspapers
- Proven digital-first workflow
- Understanding of legal compliance requirements
- Trust with solicitors and legal professionals

**Strategic Priority**: Focus on notice types that leverage our existing strengths while targeting underserved segments where traditional players struggle with digital transformation.

## 4. Detailed Notice Type Analysis

### 4.1 Probate Notices (Trustee Act s.27)

**Description & Legal Framework:**
- Required when estates >£5,000 seek protection from unknown creditors
- 2-month notice period mandated by Trustee Act 1925
- Must appear in London Gazette + local newspaper
- ~180,000 estates annually, ~60% require notices

**Current Market Players:**
- **Whitehead & Co**: £350+ per notice, slow service
- **Smythe & Co**: Regional, limited coverage
- **Direct to newspapers**: Inconsistent pricing (£150-400)

**Publication Requirements:**
- London Gazette (mandatory): £78 base rate
- Local newspaper: 1 insertion minimum
- Specific wording requirements (creditor protection clause)
- Proof of publication required

**Technical Implementation Needs:**
- Integration with London Gazette API
- Template library for standard wording
- Estate value calculator
- Automated local newspaper selection
- Compliance checking (2-month period tracking)

**Revenue Potential:**
- Market size: ~108,000 notices annually (60% of 180k estates)
- Current pricing: £200-400 per notice
- Our target pricing: £199 (all-inclusive)
- Potential revenue: £21.5M annually at 100% market share
- Realistic target (5% market share): £1.1M annually

**Why Civic Notices Wins:**
- **Price transparency**: Fixed £199 vs variable pricing elsewhere
- **Speed**: Online ordering vs phone/email chains
- **Compliance**: Built-in legal requirement checking
- **Trust**: Already used by solicitors for statutory advertising

### 4.2 Planning Notices

**Description & Legal Framework:**
- Required for planning applications under Town & Country Planning Act 1990
- Site notices + newspaper notices for certain applications
- 21-day consultation periods
- ~500,000 applications annually in England

**Current Market Players:**
- **Local planning authorities**: Internal processes, poor coordination
- **Planning consultants**: Ad-hoc newspaper relationships
- **Property developers**: Direct with newspapers
- **No dominant digital platform exists**

**Publication Requirements:**
- Local newspaper with circulation in area
- Specific timing (before determination)
- Standard wording requirements
- Site notice coordination often needed

**Technical Implementation Needs:**
- Planning portal integration possibilities
- Geographic targeting system
- Application tracking dashboard
- Developer account management
- Bulk upload capabilities

**Revenue Potential:**
- Market size: ~100,000 notices requiring newspaper publication
- Current pricing: £80-200 per notice
- Our target pricing: £99 per notice
- Potential revenue: £9.9M annually at 100% market share
- Realistic target (10% market share): £990k annually

**Why Civic Notices Wins:**
- **Speed**: Instant ordering vs council delays
- **Nationwide coverage**: Single platform vs fragmented local relationships
- **Compliance tracking**: Automated deadlines and requirements
- **Cost efficiency**: Bulk rates passed to customers

---

### 4.3 Traffic Regulation Orders

**Description & Legal Framework:**
- Required under Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984
- Local authorities must advertise proposed TROs
- 21-day objection periods
- ~25,000 TROs annually across UK

**Current Market Players:**
- **Local authority internal teams**: Manual processes
- **Highway consultants**: Limited newspaper relationships
- **Direct council-newspaper relationships**: Inefficient

**Publication Requirements:**
- Local newspaper serving affected area
- Specific legal wording requirements
- Maps/plans often required
- Follow-up notices for confirmations

**Technical Implementation Needs:**
- Council customer portal
- Map integration capabilities
- Template library for TRO types
- Objection period tracking
- Bulk processing for multiple TROs

**Revenue Potential:**
- Market size: ~25,000 notices annually
- Current pricing: £150-300 per notice
- Our target pricing: £179 per notice
- Potential revenue: £4.5M annually at 100% market share
- Realistic target (15% market share): £675k annually

**Why Civic Notices Wins:**
- **Efficiency**: Batch processing vs individual placements
- **Compliance**: Built-in legal requirement tracking
- **Cost savings**: Simplified procurement for councils
- **Transparency**: Clear audit trails for public sector

- Potential revenue: £1.5M annually at 100% market share
