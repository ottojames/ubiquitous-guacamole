# Environmental Notices (EIA) Research

## Executive Summary

Environmental Impact Assessment (EIA) notices represent a **£1.3-3.4 million annual market** in the UK. Complex regulatory requirements mandate public notification through local newspapers, creating a clear opportunity for digital disruption.

## 1. Regulatory Framework

### UK EIA Regulations 2017
- Town and Country Planning (EIA) Regulations 2017 (SI 2017 No. 571)
- Infrastructure Planning (EIA) Regulations 2017
- Environmental Permitting Regulations 2016

### Development Types Subject to EIA
- **Schedule 1**: Always require EIA (major infrastructure)
- **Schedule 2**: Require EIA if significant environmental effects

### Key Thresholds
- Industrial estates: >0.5 hectares outside sensitive areas
- Urban development: >0.5 hectares in sensitive areas, >1 hectare elsewhere
- Infrastructure: roads, railways, airports, harbours

## 2. Types of Environmental Notices

### EIA Screening Opinions
- Determine whether EIA is required
- Must be adopted within 3 weeks
- Held in public register for 2 years minimum

### EIA Scoping Opinions
- Define scope of environmental information required
- Must be adopted within 5 weeks
- Consultation with statutory consultees required

### Environmental Statements
- Report findings of completed EIA
- Must accompany planning applications
- Non-technical summary required
- Subject to mandatory public consultation

## 3. Who Publishes?

### Local Planning Authorities (348 in England)
- County councils: 26
- District councils: 164
- Unitary authorities: 56
- London boroughs: 32
- Metropolitan districts: 36
- National park authorities: 10

### Developers/Applicants
- Responsible for publishing Environmental Statement notices
- Must publish in local newspapers
- Must provide copies of environmental statements

### Environment Agency
- Issues permits for waste, water discharge, IPPC
- Publishes consultation notices for permit applications

## 4. Publication Requirements

### Mandatory Newspaper Publication (Regulation 20)
- Local newspaper circulating in the locality
- Minimum 30 days consultation period
- Must include:
  - Nature and location of development
  - Where documents can be inspected
  - How to make representations
  - Consultation deadline

### Site Notices
- Posted on or near the development site
- Visible from public highway/footpath
- Weather-resistant format

## 5. Market Size Estimate

### Annual Volumes
- Major EIA developments (Schedule 1): 50-100
- Significant Schedule 2 requiring EIA: 800-1,200
- Screening requests: 3,000-5,000
- Scoping requests: 1,000-1,500

### Cost Analysis
- Local newspaper rates: £200-£800 per notice
- Multiple insertions often required
- Average cost per EIA notice: £400-£1,200

### Market Value
- EIA planning notices: £800k-£2.4M annually
- Environmental permit notices: £500k-£1M annually
- **Combined market: £1.3-£3.4M annually**

### Related Markets
- IPPC permits: 500-800 applications annually
- Waste permits: 1,000-1,500 applications annually
- Water discharge permits: 800-1,200 applications annually
- **Total expanded market: £2-£5M annually**

## 6. Current Market Problems

1. **Limited Reach**: Declining newspaper circulation
2. **Poor Searchability**: Notices scattered across newspapers
3. **Fragmented Distribution**: No central repository
4. **High Costs**: Newspaper rates increasing
5. **Limited Accessibility**: Physical archives difficult to access
6. **No Alerting**: No way to be notified of relevant notices

## 7. Civic Notices Opportunity

### Platform Advantages
- **Centralised Repository**: All environmental notices in one place
- **Advanced Search**: Geographic, type, date filtering
- **Proactive Alerts**: Email/RSS notifications
- **Cost Efficiency**: Lower than newspaper advertising
- **Wider Reach**: National vs local circulation

### Revenue Model
- Local authorities: £50-£100 per notice
- Developers: £100-£200 per EIA notice
- Consultant subscriptions: £500-£2,000/year
- Legal firm subscriptions: £300-£1,000/year

### Target Revenue
- Year 1: 50 authorities, £200k
- Year 2: 200+ authorities, £500k ARR

### Phase 2 (Months 6-18): Publisher Adoption
- Target progressive local authorities for pilots

### Phase 3 (Months 18-36): Market Expansion
- Scale to majority of English local authorities
- Expand to Environment Agency permit notices
- Add Scottish and Welsh authorities

## 9. Competitive Landscape

### Public Notice Portal (publicnoticeportal.uk)
- Run by News Media Association
- Limited to planning notices only
- No environmental permit coverage
- Basic search functionality

### Individual Council Websites
- Fragmented across 348 authorities
- Inconsistent formats
- No cross-authority search

### Civic Notices Advantage
- Comprehensive coverage (all notice types)
- National coverage (single platform)
- Historical archive
- Professional-grade search and alerts
- Regulatory expertise
