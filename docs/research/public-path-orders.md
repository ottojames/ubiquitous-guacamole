# Public Path Orders - Research

*Research completed: January 2026*

## Overview

Public Path Orders (PPOs) are legal mechanisms in England and Wales for creating, diverting, or extinguishing public rights of way (footpaths, bridleways, and restricted byways). They represent a consistent source of statutory advertising work due to mandatory newspaper publication requirements.

---

## Legal Framework

### Primary Legislation

#### Highways Act 1980
- **Section 26** - Creation of footpaths and bridleways
- **Section 118** - Stopping up (extinguishment) of footpaths, bridleways, and restricted byways
- **Section 119** - Diversion of footpaths, bridleways, and restricted byways
- **Section 118A/119A** - Rail crossing extinguishment/diversion orders
- **Schedule 6** - Procedures for making, confirmation, validity and date of operation

#### Town and Country Planning Act 1990
- **Section 257** - Stopping up or diversion where necessary to enable development (planning permission)
- **Section 258** - Extinguishment of public rights of way over land held for planning purposes
- **Section 261** - Temporary stopping up for mineral workings
- **Schedule 14** - Procedural requirements

### Secondary Legislation

| Regulation | Scope |
|------------|-------|
| **The Public Path Orders Regulations 1993** (SI 1993/11) | Forms, notices, and procedures for Highways Act orders |
| **The Town and Country Planning (Public Path Orders) Regulations 1993** (SI 1993/10) | Forms and procedures for TCPA orders |
| **The Local Authorities (Recovery of Costs for Public Path Orders) Regulations 1993** (SI 1993/407) | Cost recovery including advertising fees |

## Statutory Advertising Requirements

### Mandatory Newspaper Publication

**Schedule 6, Highways Act 1980, Para 1(3)(a):**
> "by publication in at least one local newspaper circulating in the area in which the land to which the order relates is situated"

### Two-Stage Publication Process

PPOs require **two separate newspaper advertisements**:

1. **Notice of Making** - When the order is made and open for objections
2. **Notice of Confirmation** - After the order is confirmed (either by the authority as unopposed, or by the Secretary of State)

### Minimum Timescales

- **28 days minimum** from first publication date for representations/objections
- Service of notices must be completed **not less than 28 days** before the objection period expires

### Additional Notice Requirements

Beyond newspaper advertising, authorities must also:

- **Display notices** at:
  - Ends of affected paths
  - Council offices in the locality
  - Other appropriate places

- **Serve notices** on:
  - Owners, occupiers, and lessees of affected land
  - Parish/community councils whose area includes the land
  - Prescribed bodies (see below)
  - Diocesan Board of Finance (if ecclesiastical land)

### Prescribed Bodies (National)

The following must receive notice for **all** PPOs in England and Wales:
- Auto-Cycle Union
- British Horse Society
- Byways and Bridleways Trust
- Open Spaces Society
- Ramblers Association
- Cyclists Touring Club

**Regional bodies:**
- Peak and Northern Footpaths Society (8 northern counties)
- Chiltern Society (parts of Beds, Bucks, Herts, Oxon)
- Welsh Trail Riders' Association (Wales)

## Order-Making Authorities

### Highways Act Orders (Sections 118, 119, 26)

- **County Councils** - Highway authority for county roads
- **Unitary Authorities** - Combined functions
- **National Park Authorities** - Within National Parks
- **Broads Authority** - Within the Broads

### Town and Country Planning Act Orders (Section 257)

- **District/Borough Councils** - As local planning authority
- **County Councils** - For mineral/waste applications
- **National Park Authorities** - Within their planning areas
- **Development Corporations**

## Fees and Costs

### Local Authority Fees (2025 examples)

| Authority | Admin Fee | Notes |
|-----------|-----------|-------|
| **Cornwall Council** | £3,500 minimum | Plus advertising costs |
| **Somerset Council** | £1,500-£2,000 | Simple diversion, including adverts |
| **West Berkshire** | £1,871-£4,792 | Plus ~£300 per notice |
| **Broads Authority** | Variable | Two payments (Making + Confirmation) |

### Advertising Costs

Per the Local Authorities (Recovery of Costs) Regulations 1993:
- Authorities may recover **actual newspaper advertising costs**
- Typical range: **£300-£750 per advertisement**
- **Two advertisements required** = £600-£1,500 per order minimum
- Complex orders or multiple newspapers can exceed £1,500 total

### Statutory Maximum (Admin Costs Only)

The 1993 Regulations cap admin cost recovery at:
- **£400** for orders relating to one path
- **+£75** for each additional path included

*Note: These 1993 caps are outdated; advertising costs are passed through at actual cost.*

## Market Opportunity

### Volume Indicators

PPOs are made by:
- **152 Highway Authorities** in England and Wales
- **~300+ Local Planning Authorities** (for TCPA orders)
- Thousands of orders annually across all authorities

### Demand Drivers

1. **Housing development** - Most new housing affects existing footpaths
2. **Infrastructure projects** - Roads, railways, utilities
3. **Agricultural rationalisation** - Farmers wanting paths moved
4. **Estate management** - Privacy/security concerns
5. **Safety improvements** - Dangerous crossings, unstable paths
6. **Development pressure** - Ongoing house-building targets

### Competitive Landscape

PPO advertising is currently handled by:
- Local newspapers (incumbent, but declining)
- Regional newspaper groups
- Some specialist advertisers

### Opportunity Assessment

**Strengths for market entry:**
- Mandatory legal requirement (cannot be avoided)
- Two advertisements per order (doubled revenue per order)
- High repeat rate (authorities make multiple orders per year)
- Predictable, standardised format
- Authorities seek cost-effective solutions
- Declining local newspaper industry creates uncertainty

**Challenges:**
- Requirement for "local newspaper circulating in the area"
- Some authorities have established relationships
- Relatively small individual transaction values
- Fragmented customer base (many authorities)

### Pricing Opportunity

Current market pricing appears to be:
- **£300-750** per advertisement
- **£600-1,500** per complete order (two ads)

For comparison with other statutory notices:
- Licensing notices: £100-300 per ad
- Planning notices: Variable
- Deceased estates: £100-200 per ad

**PPO advertising is at the higher end of statutory notice pricing.**

## Notice Content Requirements

### Notice of Making (Form 1 from SI 1993/11)

Must state:
- The general effect of the order
- That the order has been made and is about to be submitted for confirmation
- Place where the order and map can be inspected free of charge
- Time period for objections (minimum 28 days from first publication)
- How to make representations/objections

### Notice of Confirmation (Form 3 from SI 1993/11)

Must state:
- That the order has been confirmed (with/without modifications)
- The effect of the order
- That it may be challenged in the High Court within 6 weeks
- Where documents can be inspected

### Map Requirement

All orders must contain a map at:
- **Minimum scale 1:2500**
- Or largest scale readily available if 1:2500 not available

## Process Timeline

```
Application received
        ↓
Authority investigation (variable: weeks to months)
        ↓
Decision to make order
        ↓
ORDER MADE → NEWSPAPER NOTICE #1 (Notice of Making)
        ↓
28-day objection period
        ↓
If objections → Public inquiry/hearing → Secretary of State decision
If no objections → Authority confirms as unopposed
        ↓
ORDER CONFIRMED → NEWSPAPER NOTICE #2 (Notice of Confirmation)
        ↓
6-week challenge period
        ↓
Order comes into force
```

Typical end-to-end: **6-18 months** (simple unopposed order: 3-6 months)

## Summary for Civic Notices Business

**Public Path Orders represent a solid market opportunity:**

1. ✅ **Mandatory** - Legal requirement for newspaper advertising
2. ✅ **Two ads per order** - Doubles revenue vs single-notice requirements  
3. ✅ **Higher pricing** - £300-750 per ad is premium end of market
4. ✅ **Repeat business** - Authorities make multiple orders per year
5. ✅ **Standardised format** - Low complexity to fulfil
6. ⚠️ **Fragmented** - Many small authorities as customers
7. ⚠️ "Local newspaper" - Must meet circulation requirement

**Recommended actions:**
- Target county councils (larger volumes)
- Target planning authorities in high-development areas
- Bundle with other statutory advertising services (licensing, planning)
- Consider partnerships with local newspaper groups for distribution
