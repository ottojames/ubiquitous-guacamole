fix(gambling): add licensing objectives statement to all 16 gambling templates (CRIT-004)

STATUTORY COMPLIANCE FIX - Gambling Act 2005

Issue:
- All 16 gambling templates missing statement about licensing objectives
- No guidance provided to representors on grounds for valid representations

Statutory Requirement:
- Gambling Act 2005 s.1 - Three licensing objectives apply to all premises licence applications
- Licensing objectives are mandatory consideration for representations

Changes:
Added licensing objectives statement to all 16 gambling templates:
- gambling-betting-new
- gambling-betting-variation
- gambling-betting-review
- gambling-betting-transfer
- gambling-bingo-new
- gambling-bingo-variation
- gambling-bingo-review
- gambling-bingo-transfer
- gambling-agc-new
- gambling-agc-variation
- gambling-agc-review
- gambling-agc-transfer
- gambling-fec-new
- gambling-fec-variation
- gambling-fec-review
- gambling-fec-transfer

New Statement Added (between premises description and inspection details):
"Any representations must relate to one or more of the licensing objectives under the Gambling Act 2005: (a) preventing gambling from being a source of crime or disorder, being associated with crime or disorder, or being used to support crime; (b) ensuring that gambling is conducted in a fair and open way; (c) protecting children and other vulnerable persons from being harmed or exploited by gambling."

Location in Templates:
- "New" templates: Inserted after premises/hours description, before inspection location
- "Variation" templates: Inserted after variation description, before "Inspection and representation details as above"
- "Review" templates: Inserted after review grounds, before "Inspection and representation details as above"
- "Transfer" templates: Inserted after transfer details, before "Inspection and representation details as above"

Legal Risk Eliminated:
- Representors now informed of statutory grounds for valid representations
- Reduces risk of irrelevant representations that must be administratively filtered
- Ensures councils receive focused, licensing-objective-related representations
- Aligns with Gambling Commission guidance on consultation

Files Changed:
- src/next/publish/templates/gambling.ts (16 templates updated)

Implementation Method:
- Manual insertion into each of 16 templates
- Statement placed logically between application details and procedural information
- Consistent wording across all variants

Testing: See patches/CRIT-004-validation.md
