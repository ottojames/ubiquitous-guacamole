fix(licensing): add responsible authorities statement to all templates and schema (CRIT-002)

STATUTORY COMPLIANCE FIX - Licensing Act 2003

Issue:
- All 6 licensing templates missing statement that representors must serve copies on responsible authorities
- Schema lacked RESPONSIBLE_AUTHORITIES_LIST_URL field for optional URL to authorities list

Statutory Requirement:
- Licensing Act 2003 s.17(5)(b) - Notice must facilitate service on responsible authorities
- Licensing Act 2003 s.13 - Definition of responsible authorities
- Licensing Act 2003 s.17(3)(b) - Representors must serve responsible authorities

Changes:

Schema (src/next/publish/schema/licensing.ts):
- Added RESPONSIBLE_AUTHORITIES_LIST_URL field (optional URL)
- Uses existing optionalUrl() validator
- Allows councils to provide link to their responsible authorities list

Templates (src/next/publish/templates/licensing.ts):
All 6 templates updated with new statement:
- licensing-premises-new
- licensing-premises-variation
- licensing-premises-review
- licensing-club-new
- licensing-club-variation
- licensing-club-review

New wording structure:
"Any representations must be made in writing to {{AUTHORITY_NAME}} at {{REPRESENTATION_ADDRESS}}
 {{#if REPRESENTATION_EMAIL}} or {{REPRESENTATION_EMAIL}}{{/if}} by {{DEADLINE_DATE}}.
 Representors must also serve a copy of their representations on each of the responsible authorities
 {{#if RESPONSIBLE_AUTHORITIES_LIST_URL}} (the list is available at {{RESPONSIBLE_AUTHORITIES_LIST_URL}} or from the licensing authority){{/if}}."

Features:
- Mandatory statement about serving responsible authorities
- Optional conditional display of URL if council provides one
- Generic statement if URL not provided (users can get list from authority)
- Changed "{{REPRESENTATION_METHOD}}" to explicit "in writing" for clarity

UI Note:
- Form input for RESPONSIBLE_AUTHORITIES_LIST_URL field should be added to notice creation form (wizard step)
- Field should be optional text input with URL validation
- Help text: "Provide link where representors can view responsible authorities list"

Legal Risk Eliminated:
- Notices now facilitate service on responsible authorities per s.17(5)(b)
- Reduces risk of judicial review for failure to comply with consultation requirements

Files Changed:
- src/next/publish/schema/licensing.ts (+1 field)
- src/next/publish/templates/licensing.ts (6 templates updated)

Testing: See patches/CRIT-002-validation.md
