fix(probate): add complete Trustee Act s.27 protection wording (CRIT-007)

STATUTORY COMPLIANCE FIX - Trustee Act 1925

Issue:
- Probate template missing explicit statutory reference in notice body
- Protection clause incomplete - missing full liability protection language
- Abbreviated wording insufficient for professional legal practice standards

Statutory Requirement:
- Trustee Act 1925 s.27(2) - Protection for personal representatives
- Professional solicitors expect full statutory protection wording
- Incomplete protection language may expose personal representatives to claims

Changes:

1. Notice Body Enhancement (line 13):
BEFORE:
"NOTICE is hereby given that any persons having claims..."

AFTER:
"NOTICE is hereby given pursuant to section 27 of the Trustee Act 1925 that any persons having claims..."

- Explicitly invokes s.27 in notice body
- Demonstrates statutory basis for protection

2. Protection Clause Enhancement (line 15):
BEFORE:
"After this date the estate may be distributed having regard only to the claims of which notice has been received."

AFTER:
"After this date the personal representatives will distribute the estate among the persons entitled thereto having regard only to the claims and interests of which they have had notice and will not be liable for the assets of the estate or any part thereof so distributed to any person of whose claims or interests they have not had notice at the time of distribution."

- Added explicit statement about personal representatives
- Added "distribute the estate among the persons entitled thereto" (identifies beneficiaries)
- Added "claims and interests" (covers both creditors and beneficiaries)
- Added CRITICAL liability protection: "will not be liable for the assets of the estate or any part thereof so distributed to any person of whose claims or interests they have not had notice at the time of distribution"

Legal Effect:
- Full s.27(2) protection language now present
- Personal representatives protected from unknown claims after distribution
- Wording matches professional legal practice standards
- Notice now provides complete statutory shield

Legal Risk Eliminated:
- Personal representatives now have full statutory protection under Trustee Act 1925 s.27(2)
- Solicitors can confidently advise clients that notice provides complete protection
- Reduces risk of claims against personal representatives after estate distribution
- Aligns with Law Society and professional best practice

- Enhanced existing template with professional statutory wording
- Two specific changes: notice invocation + liability protection clause
