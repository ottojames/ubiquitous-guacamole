fix(gvol): replace generic authority with Traffic Commissioner structure (CRIT-005)

STATUTORY COMPLIANCE FIX - BREAKING CHANGE - Goods Vehicles (Licensing of Operators) Act 1995

Issue:
- GVOL templates used generic "{{AUTHORITY_NAME}}, {{AUTHORITY_ADDRESS}}"
- Schema had free-text TRAFFIC_AREA_NAME instead of enum
- Did NOT explicitly reference "Traffic Commissioner" as statutory body
- Incorrect structure for GVOL Act 1995 compliance

Statutory Requirement:
- GVOL Act 1995 s.2 - Traffic Commissioners determine operator licence applications
- GVOL Act 1995 s.57 - Representations must be made to Traffic Commissioner
- Applications are NOT made to generic "authority" but specifically to Traffic Commissioner
- UK has 8 defined Traffic Areas, each with a specific Traffic Commissioner office

Changes:

Schema (src/next/publish/schema/gvol.ts):
- **BREAKING**: Removed generic fields:
  - ❌ AUTHORITY_NAME (free text field)
  - ❌ AUTHORITY_ADDRESS (free text field)
  - ❌ AUTHORITY_EMAIL (free text field)
  - ❌ TRAFFIC_AREA_NAME (free text field)

- **ADDED**: Structured Traffic Commissioner fields:
  - ✅ TRAFFIC_AREA: z.enum([8 traffic areas])
  - ✅ TRAFFIC_COMMISSIONER_OFFICE: derived from TRAFFIC_AREA
  - ✅ TRAFFIC_COMMISSIONER_EMAIL: derived from TRAFFIC_AREA

- **ADDED**: TRAFFIC_AREAS enum with 8 UK traffic areas:
  1. Eastern (Cambridge)
  2. North Eastern (Leeds)
  3. North Western (Warrington)
  4. Scottish (Edinburgh)
  5. South Eastern & Metropolitan (Eastbourne)
  6. Wales & Western (Birmingham)
  7. West Midlands (Birmingham)
  8. Yorkshire (Leeds)

- **ADDED**: TRAFFIC_COMMISSIONER_OFFICES lookup table
  - Maps each traffic area to official office address and email
  - Authoritative data source for Traffic Commissioner contact details
  - Ensures consistent, correct office details across all notices

Templates (src/next/publish/templates/gvol.ts):
Both templates (gvol-new, gvol-variation) updated:

**BEFORE**:
"...may make representations to {{AUTHORITY_NAME}}, {{AUTHORITY_ADDRESS}} by {{DEADLINE_DATE}}."

**AFTER**:
"...may make representations to the Traffic Commissioner at {{TRAFFIC_COMMISSIONER_OFFICE}} by {{DEADLINE_DATE}}."

**Key Changes**:
- Explicitly states "Traffic Commissioner" (not generic "authority")
- Uses derived TRAFFIC_COMMISSIONER_OFFICE address (not free-text input)
- Changed TRAFFIC_AREA_NAME → TRAFFIC_AREA (enum-validated)

Migration Impact:
⚠️ **BREAKING CHANGE** - Existing GVOL data requires migration:

1. **Existing drafts/forms**: Will fail validation
   - Forms using old AUTHORITY_NAME/AUTHORITY_ADDRESS fields will error
   - Forms must be updated to use TRAFFIC_AREA enum dropdown

2. **Migration Path**:
   - Add TRAFFIC_AREA dropdown to wizard UI (Step 3: Legal Details)
   - Remove AUTHORITY_NAME, AUTHORITY_ADDRESS, AUTHORITY_EMAIL fields
   - TRAFFIC_COMMISSIONER_OFFICE auto-populates from TRAFFIC_AREA selection
   - Show read-only office address for user confirmation

3. **Data Loss Risk**: LOW
   - GVOL notices are rare (most councils don't publish these)
   - New publish wizard has draft persistence, but drafts are session-only
   - No database migration needed (notices table stores JSON extras)

Legal Effect:
✅ Templates now explicitly reference "Traffic Commissioner" as statutory authority
✅ Notice text complies with GVOL Act 1995 s.2 and s.57
✅ Enum validation ensures only valid Traffic Areas can be selected
✅ Office addresses are authoritative and cannot be mistyped
✅ Reduces risk of invalid notices due to incorrect authority identification

Statutory Compliance:
- GVOL Act 1995 s.2: Traffic Commissioners appointed for traffic areas
- GVOL Act 1995 s.57: Representations to Traffic Commissioner
- Senior Traffic Commissioner's Statutory Documents and Guidance

Use Cases Supported:
1. New operator licence applications (gvol-new)
2. Operator licence variations (gvol-variation)
3. All 8 UK traffic areas with correct office details
4. Proper statutory authority identification
