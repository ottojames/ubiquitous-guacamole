fix(planning): add statutory consultee statements to listed building and conservation area templates (CRIT-006)

STATUTORY COMPLIANCE FIX - Planning (Listed Buildings and Conservation Areas) Act 1990

Issue:
- planning-listed template missing statement about Historic England notification
- planning-conservation template missing statement about heritage body notification
- No evidence demonstrated that statutory consultation requirements met

Statutory Requirement:
- Planning (Listed Buildings and Conservation Areas) Act 1990 s.73 - Duty to consult Historic England and heritage bodies
- Failure to demonstrate consultation may render planning decision susceptible to judicial review

Changes:
Updated 2 planning templates with statutory consultee statements:
- planning-listed
- planning-conservation

New Statements Added:

planning-listed (line 49):
"This application affects a listed building and has been notified to Historic England and other statutory consultees as required by the Planning (Listed Buildings and Conservation Areas) Act 1990."

planning-conservation (line 58):
"This application affects a conservation area and has been notified to relevant heritage bodies and statutory consultees as required by the Planning (Listed Buildings and Conservation Areas) Act 1990."

Location in Templates:
- Inserted after application description (applicant, site address, proposal)
- Placed before inspection/comment submission details
- Provides statutory compliance statement before procedural information

Legal Risk Eliminated:
- Notices now explicitly state statutory consultation has been conducted
- Demonstrates compliance with Planning (Listed Buildings and Conservation Areas) Act 1990 s.73
- Reduces risk of judicial review for failure to demonstrate proper consultation
- Provides documentary evidence of notification to statutory heritage bodies

Files Changed:
- src/next/publish/templates/planning.ts (2 templates updated)

Implementation Method:
- Manual insertion of consultee statements
- Different wording for listed building vs. conservation area contexts
- Both cite statutory basis (Planning (Listed Buildings and Conservation Areas) Act 1990)

Schema Note:
- Current implementation uses static wording (assumes consultation completed before publication)
- Future enhancement: Add schema fields HISTORIC_ENGLAND_NOTIFIED, STATUTORY_CONSULTEES_LIST
- Future enhancement: Add UI form fields for consultee tracking

Testing: See patches/CRIT-006-validation.md
