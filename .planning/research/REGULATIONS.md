# UK Public Notice Regulatory Landscape

**Domain:** Statutory Public Notice Publication
**Researched:** 2026-01-22
**Overall Confidence:** HIGH (verified via legislation.gov.uk and official sources)

## Executive Summary

UK statutory public notices are governed by multiple legislative frameworks, each with specific publication requirements, consultation periods, and compliance obligations. The current system mandates newspaper publication for most notice types, though government reform proposals (Rachel Reeves, July 2025) signal potential regulatory change that could enable digital-first alternatives.

This document catalogues the regulatory requirements a digital public notice platform must satisfy to replace or supplement traditional newspaper publication.

---

## Part 1: Notice Types and Statutory Requirements

### 1.1 Licensing Act 2003 (Premises Licences)

**Primary Legislation:** Licensing Act 2003, Section 17
**Secondary Legislation:** The Licensing Act 2003 (Premises Licences and Club Premises Certificates) Regulations 2005 (S.I. 2005/42)

#### Publication Requirements (Regulation 25)

| Requirement | Specification |
|-------------|---------------|
| **Newspaper Notice** | Published in a local newspaper (or local newsletter if no newspaper) circulating in the vicinity of the premises |
| **Publication Window** | At least once within 10 working days starting the day after application submission |
| **Site Notice** | Pale blue, minimum A4 size, 16pt black font, displayed prominently where readable from outside |
| **Site Notice Duration** | 28 consecutive days starting day after application |
| **Large Premises** | Additional notices every 50 metres along external perimeter if premises exceed 50 square metres frontage |

#### Consultation Period
- **28 days** from application submission
- Representations must address one or more licensing objectives:
  - Prevention of crime and disorder
  - Public safety
  - Prevention of public nuisance
  - Protection of children from harm

#### Responsible Authorities Notified
- Police
- Fire and Rescue
- Environmental Health
- Child Protection
- Planning Authority
- Trading Standards
- Public Health

#### Application Types Requiring Advertising
- New premises licence (Section 17)
- Provisional statement (Section 29)
- Licence variation (Section 34)
- Club premises certificate (Section 71)
- Certificate variation (Section 84)

**Statutory Reference:** [Regulation 25, S.I. 2005/42](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2005/42/regulation/25)

### 1.2 Gambling Act 2005 (Gambling Premises)

**Primary Legislation:** Gambling Act 2005
**Secondary Legislation:** The Gambling Act 2005 (Premises Licences and Provisional Statements) Regulations 2007 (S.I. 2007/459)

#### Publication Requirements

| Requirement | Specification |
|-------------|---------------|
| **Newspaper Notice** | Published in a local newspaper (or local newsletter if none) circulating in the area |
| **Publication Window** | At least once within 10 working days starting day after application |
| **Site Notice** | White background, prominently displayed where readable by passers-by |
| **Site Notice Duration** | 28 consecutive days minimum |
| **Large Premises** | Notice every 50 metres if frontage exceeds 50 metres |

#### Consultation Period
- **28 days** for representations from interested parties and responsible authorities

#### Notice Content Requirements
- Postal address of licensing authority
- Web address where register is kept
- Date by which representations must be made
- Statement that false/misleading information is an offence under Section 342

#### Licence Types
- Casino premises licence
- Bingo premises licence
- Adult gaming centre
- Family entertainment centre
- Betting premises licence (general/track)

**Statutory Reference:** [Gambling Act 2005 (Premises Licences) Regulations 2007](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2007/459/contents/made)

### 1.3 Town and Country Planning (Development Management)

**Primary Legislation:** Town and Country Planning Act 1990
**Secondary Legislation:** Town and Country Planning (Development Management Procedure) (England) Order 2015 (S.I. 2015/595)

#### Publication Requirements (Article 15)

| Application Type | Site Notice | Newspaper | Notice Period |
|------------------|-------------|-----------|---------------|
| Major developments (10+ dwellings, 1000m²+ commercial) | Required | Required | 21 days |
| Listed building consent | Required | Required | 21 days |
| Conservation area works | Required | Required | 21 days |
| EIA developments | Required | Required | 21 days |
| Departure from development plan | Required | Required | 21 days |
| Standard applications | Required | Optional | 21 days |

#### Notice to Owners (Article 13)
Applicants must serve notice on land owners/tenants:
- By direct service on known persons
- By newspaper publication in locality
- By site display for minimum 7 days in 21-day period before application

#### Consultation Period
- **21 days** minimum from site notice display
- Planning applications cannot be determined until consultation period expires

#### Statutory Consultees
- Natural England
- Historic England
- Environment Agency
- Highways England
- Health and Safety Executive
- Local Highway Authority
- Relevant parish/town councils

**Statutory Reference:** [DMPO 2015](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2015/595)

### 1.4 Traffic Regulation Orders (TROs)

**Primary Legislation:** Road Traffic Regulation Act 1984
**Secondary Legislation:** The Local Authorities' Traffic Orders (Procedure) (England and Wales) Regulations 1996

#### Publication Requirements

| Stage | Requirement | Timing |
|-------|-------------|--------|
| **Notice of Proposals** | Published in local newspaper | Before making order |
| **On-site Notices** | Displayed in affected roads | During consultation |
| **Notice of Making** | Published in local newspaper | Within 14 days of making order |

#### Consultation Period
- **21 days** minimum for objections to Notice of Proposals
- Order must be made within 2 years of Notice of Proposals publication

#### TRO Types and Requirements

| TRO Type | Prior Consultation | Newspaper Notice |
|----------|-------------------|------------------|
| Permanent TRO | Yes (21 days) | Yes (Intention + Making) |
| Experimental TRO | No | Yes (Making only) |
| Temporary TRO (>2 weeks) | Not required | Yes |
| Temporary TRO (<=2 weeks) | Not required | Not required |

#### Notice Content
- Name of order-making authority
- Title of proposed order
- Brief statement of nature and effect
- Description of affected roads
- Location where documents available for inspection

**Statutory Reference:** [Local Authorities' Traffic Orders (Procedure) Regulations 1996](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1996/2489)

### 1.5 Probate / Deceased Estates (Trustee Act 1925)

**Primary Legislation:** Trustee Act 1925, Section 27

#### Publication Requirements

| Requirement | Specification |
|-------------|---------------|
| **The Gazette** | Notice published in London, Edinburgh, or Belfast Gazette |
| **Newspaper** | If deceased owned land/property, also publish in local newspaper circulating where property situated |

#### Claims Period
- **2 months and 1 day** from publication date in England/Wales
- Creditors must come forward within this period

#### Pre-requisites for Publishing
- Grant of Probate, or
- Grant of Letters of Administration, or
- Death certificate

#### Protection Offered
- Personal representatives not liable for unknown creditors who appear after claims period
- Does not prevent claims being made against estate
- Late creditors must seek recovery from beneficiaries, not PRs

#### Volume (2024 data)
- 31,000 probate notices published in newspapers
- 41,000 notices published in The Gazette

**Statutory Reference:** [Trustee Act 1925, Section 27](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/Geo5/15-16/19/section/27)

### 1.6 Goods Vehicle Operator Licensing (GVOL)

**Regulatory Body:** Traffic Commissioner
**Legislation:** Goods Vehicles (Licensing of Operators) Act 1995

#### Publication Requirements

| Requirement | Specification |
|-------------|---------------|
| **Newspaper Notice** | Published in local newspaper where operating centre located |
| **Submission Window** | Application must be submitted within 21 days of notice publication |
| **Exemptions** | Light goods vehicles (vans) do not require newspaper notice |
| **PSV Licences** | No newspaper notice required |

#### Notice Content
- Details of proposed operating centre
- Number and type of vehicles
- Applicant details

#### Typical Costs
- Newspaper notice: GBP 200-300
- Total licence application: approximately GBP 1,000

### 1.7 Insolvency Notices

**Primary Legislation:** Insolvency Act 1986
**Secondary Legislation:** Insolvency (England and Wales) Rules 2016

#### Gazette Publication (Mandatory)
The following must be published in The London Gazette:
- Winding-up orders
- Bankruptcy orders
- Appointment of administrators/liquidators
- Company voluntary arrangements
- Individual voluntary arrangements

#### Newspaper Publication (Discretionary since April 2009)
- No automatic requirement for newspaper publication
- Office-holder's discretion in specific cases:
  - Non-surrender cases
  - Non-trace cases
  - Non-cooperation cases
  - Suspected non-disclosure

#### Standard Notice Contents
- Name and contact details of office-holder
- Capacity in which acting
- Date of appointment
- Company identification details
- Prior names (12 months before proceedings)

**Statutory Reference:** [Insolvency (England and Wales) Rules 2016](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2016/1024)

---

### 1.8 Deed Poll (Enrolled Name Changes)

**Legislation:** The Enrolment of Deeds (Change of Name) Regulations 1994

#### Publication Requirements

| Type | Gazette Publication | Newspaper |
|------|---------------------|-----------|
| Enrolled deed poll | Mandatory (London Gazette) | Not required |
| Unenrolled deed poll | Not required | Not required |

#### Key Points
- Enrolment is voluntary (less than 1% of name changes are enrolled)
- Unenrolled deed polls are legally equivalent
- Enrolled deeds create public record at Royal Courts of Justice
- Cost: GBP 53.05 for enrolment

**Note:** This is low priority for a civic notices platform as most name changes do not require statutory publication.

## Part 2: Regulatory Reform Landscape

### 2.1 Rachel Reeves Proposal (July 2025)

**Announcement:** The Mail on Sunday, 27 July 2025

#### Proposed Changes
- Remove requirement for businesses to advertise licensing applications in locally printed press
- Part of broader "red tape bonfire" to help hospitality sector
- Aims to make it easier for cafes and bars to open on high streets

#### Current Status
- No legislative timeline announced
- Industry opposition from News Media Association and Society of Editors
- DCMS Committee previously recommended review in 2023 (no action taken)

#### Industry Concerns
- "Betrayal of local communities and the public's right to know" - NMA
- 10 million people (elderly/vulnerable) rely on print newspapers for notices
- Revenue loss would harm local journalism
- Risk of notices being diverted to fake local news platforms

**Confidence:** MEDIUM - Proposal announced but no concrete legislation or timeline

---

### 2.2 Digital First Precedents

#### Public Notice Portal (News Media Association)
- Launched 2023 with GBP 1m Google News Initiative funding
- Aggregates statutory notices from local newspapers
- Won Digital Initiative of the Year 2024
- Does not replace statutory newspaper requirement
- Available at [publicnoticeportal.uk](https://publicnoticeportal.uk)

#### LGA Survey 2023 Findings
- Survey conducted January-February 2023
- Councils seeking reform of statutory notice requirements
- Digital publication preferred for cost savings

#### US State Precedents
- Virginia (2024): HB 264 allows digital-only publishers to post public notices
- Requirements: 2+ years registration, local news staff, dedicated domain, no paywall
- Florida (2023): Allowed county website publication, but research showed decreased public participation

## Part 3: Compliance Requirements for Digital Platforms

### 3.1 Current Legal Position

Digital-only platforms **cannot** currently satisfy statutory newspaper publication requirements for:
- Licensing Act 2003 notices
- Gambling Act 2005 notices
- Planning notices requiring newspaper advertisement
- TRO notices
- GVOL notices

The legislation specifies "local newspaper" or "local newspaper or newsletter" with no provision for digital-only publication.

### 3.2 Platform Requirements (If Legislation Changes)

A compliant digital public notice platform would need:

#### Publication Verification
| Requirement | Rationale |
|-------------|-----------|
| **Timestamp proof** | Evidence of publication date for statutory deadlines |
| **Immutable record** | Cannot alter notice after publication |
| **Geographic targeting** | "Circulating in the vicinity" equivalent |
| **Accessibility** | Available to affected communities |
| **Audit trail** | Proof of compliance for legal proceedings |

#### Content Requirements
| Element | Standard |
|---------|----------|
| **Applicant details** | Full name, address, contact |
| **Premises/location** | Full address, grid reference |
| **Application type** | Clear description of proposal |
| **Objection deadline** | Calculated from publication date |
| **Authority contact** | Where to submit representations |
| **Licensing objectives** | (For licensing notices) |

#### Technical Requirements
- HTTPS encryption
- Geographic reach verification
- Publication date certification
- Archive retention (minimum: consultation period + appeals period + 6 years)
- Accessibility compliance (WCAG 2.2 recommended)
- Search functionality by location, date, type

### 3.3 Evidence of Publication

Courts and licensing authorities require proof that notice was:
1. Published in correct publication
2. Published within statutory timeframe
3. Contained required information
4. Was accessible to affected persons

Current newspaper proof: copy of publication + affidavit
Digital equivalent needed: timestamped record, publication certificate, access logs

## Part 5: Strategic Implications

### 5.1 Immediate Opportunity

**Licensing Act 2003 notices** are the primary target:
- Government has announced reform intention
- 28-day consultation period is well-defined
- Clear statutory content requirements
- High volume (every pub, restaurant, club, off-licence application)
- Geographic targeting is straightforward (premises location)

### 5.2 Near-Term Expansion

**Planning notices** are secondary:
- Reform discussions ongoing
- More complex (multiple application types)
- Longer approval chain (statutory consultees)
- Higher public interest

### 5.3 Regulatory Watch Items

| Development | Impact | Timeline |
|-------------|--------|----------|
| Licensing Act newspaper requirement removal | Enables digital-first for premises licences | Unknown (announced July 2025) |
| TRO reform consultation (DfT 2022) | Potential digital publication for traffic orders | Ongoing |
| LGA statutory notices reform | Broader council notice digitisation | Lobbying active |

### 5.4 Compliance Architecture

The platform should be built to satisfy these compliance principles from day one:

1. **Publication certainty** - Cryptographic proof of publication timestamp
2. **Geographic reach** - Demonstrable coverage of "vicinity"
3. **Accessibility** - Available to non-technical users (no app required)
4. **Permanence** - Archive for full limitation period
5. **Auditability** - Complete chain of custody for legal proceedings
